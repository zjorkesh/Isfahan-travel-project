Why Isfahan?

Isfahan, The Heart of Persian Art and Culture

Isfahan is a city where history, art, and everyday life come together. With its beautiful architecture, historic bridges, colorful bazaars, and famous Persian gardens, there is always something interesting to discover. It also has four distinct seasons, and its autumn is especially beautiful, with colorful leaves and pleasant weather. For me, Isfahan is special because it combines the beauty of the past with the energy of modern city life.

My favorite restaurant in Isfahan

Shahrzad Restaurant

Renowned establishment in Isfahan, known for its exquisite Persian cuisine and elegant ambiance. The restaurant features stunning mirror work and intricate decorations, creating a regal atmosphere that reflects Iranian artistic heritage.

Adrress:

Abbas Abad Street, Isfahan 8134616811 Iran

What I like about it

The moment you step inside, you are greeted by stunning mirror work that adorns the walls and ceilings, creating a dazzling, almost regal ambiance. The restaurant’s decor reflects the rich artistic heritage of Iran, making every meal feel like a special occasion.

Ghasr Monshi Restaurant

Traditional restaurant offering Persian Cuisine/ Buffet consisting veggie and non veggie items. It is 5-10 minutes walk away from famous Imam square (located at Ghasr Monshi Hotel). Because the food prepared fresh everyday, we prepare based on the number of reservations.

Adrress:

Neshat str West Ghasr Monshi Alley, Isfahan 81464 Iran

What I like about it

It’s a great place with unique atmosphere. Many workers speaking English and it helps a lot. Foods are delicious and service fast. Great place with great workers.

Azam Beryani

Popular restaurant chain in Isfahan, known for serving traditional Isfahani Beryani. This dish features minced mutton or lamb skewered and cooked over an open fire, then served on a piece of Sangak bread and topped with chopped almonds and barberries. The food is rich and fatty, making it a hearty main course.

Adrress:

Masjed Seyyed Street Nearby Masjed Seyyed Post-Office, Isfahan 81464 Iran

What I like about it

Azam Beryani restaurant has a secret recipe for Biryani and prepares this dish with fresh ingredients such as minced lamb. This dish serves with fresh Sangak bread, Doogh, and various side dishes.
